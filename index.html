Dohyun Lee

Ph.D. Student, KAIST AI

Hi! I am a Ph.D. student at KAIST AI (DAVIAN Lab), advised by Jaegul Choo.

My current research interest lies in how robots can build and use an understanding of the 3D world to manipulate objects and act in real environments. My research centers on two directions: (i) bringing the 3D scene understanding of vision-language models to robot manipulation through explicit 3D spatial reasoning, and (ii) treating video generation as representation learning for the 3D world and its dynamics, to find what learning objectives yield good representations for world action models.
