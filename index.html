About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at the Data and Visual Analytics Lab (DAVIAN) in KAIST AI, advised by Professor Jaegul Choo. Previously, I was a research intern at KRAFTON.

My current research interest lies in how robots can build and use an understanding of the 3D world to manipulate objects and act in real environments. My research centers on two directions. First, while building PUBG Ally at KRAFTON, I saw first-hand that vision-language models understand complex 3D scenes remarkably well, and I now aim to bring this capability to robot manipulation through explicit 3D spatial reasoning. Second, video is another rich source of 3D world understanding. I believe that video generation is essentially representation learning for the 3D world and its dynamics, and I am currently studying what learning objectives yield good representations for world action models.
